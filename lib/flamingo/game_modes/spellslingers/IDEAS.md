# Spellslingers: ideas for later

Not built yet. Parked here so we can pick them up once the core feel is right.

## Mana instead of one cast per turn (Arcanists-style)

Each wizard gets a mana pool per turn, and every spell has a cost. A turn becomes
a choice: one big spell, or two small ones (say a ward plus an ember bolt).
Questions to settle first:

- Does mana carry over between turns, so saving up for an Inferno is a play?
- Do mana crates replace the current mana-crate effect (cooldown refresh)?
- Do quick spells stay "free", or just become cheap spells?

## More camera work

The camera follows shots and knockbacks and pans at the screen edge. Still open:

- Zoom in on big impacts and knockouts, zoom out while aiming long shots.
- A short "here's everyone" sweep across the map at the start of the duel.

## Movement

- Jump and backflip, with fall damage, so terrain shape matters more.
- Retreat time: a few seconds to walk after casting, like Worms.
