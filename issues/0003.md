## What to build

Keep the completed drawing visible behind the post-round reveal and score UI instead of replacing it with a separate full-screen results state. Move the revealed word above the canvas area and present the round-end overlay with partial opacity so players can still read the final image underneath while the word reveal and score updates are shown.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] When a round ends, the finished drawing remains visible behind the reveal and score UI instead of disappearing before the next turn.
- [ ] The revealed word is displayed above the canvas in the round-end overlay layout.
- [ ] The round-end overlay is translucent enough that the final drawing remains visible while the reveal and score information are shown.

None - can start immediately
