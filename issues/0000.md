## What to build

Make round audio follow the actual game flow end to end. Background music should play during the drawing and guessing phase, fade out during word choice and round reveal, and fade back in when drawing resumes. Add the final 10-second countdown cue during the drawing phase, ensuring it starts once per turn, stops cleanly if the turn ends early, and does not leak across phase transitions or reconnects.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] During the `WordChoice` and `Guessing` flow, music only plays during the active drawing and guessing phase and fades out during word choice and round reveal transitions.
- [ ] A countdown sound plays for the final 10 seconds of a drawing round and stops immediately if the round ends before the timer expires.
- [ ] Rejoining, advancing phases, or ending a game does not leave music or countdown audio stuck playing across screens.

None - can start immediately
